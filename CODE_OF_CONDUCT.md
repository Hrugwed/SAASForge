# Code of conduct

SaaSForge is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent while addressing concrete impact, respect privacy, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest avoided where possible.
